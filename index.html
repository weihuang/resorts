Five Caribbean all-inclusives for Christmas with a 2½-year-old

About 60 resorts were screened. Most won’t look after a child under 3 or 4 unless a parent stays. These five have staffed care built for under-3s, new or recently renovated family rooms and a real sand beach. Each one has fine print to settle in writing before you pay a deposit.

The short answer

Beaches Turks & Caicos if its nursery says yes, Casa de Campo if it doesn’t

Ranked on your five requirements in the order you gave them, with bad reviews counted against each resort.

  1. 1

    Best overall, if the nursery is confirmed

    Beaches Turks & Caicos

    Grace Bay, Providenciales · Treasure Beach Village

    Free nursery · age wording unclearNew suites · Mar 2026

    The newest family suites in the field, Grace Bay’s calm powder sand, and butler suites whose listing includes a private airport car for the 15–20 minute ride.

    Watch out: the free nursery is described as “ages 0–2” and as “up to 24 months”, so get written confirmation for a 30-month-old in diapers. It ranks only #34 of 52 Providenciales hotels on TripAdvisor, and a 100-room wing is being rebuilt into early 2027.

  2. 2

    Surest toddler care

    Casa de Campo

    La Romana, Dominican Republic · Casa Inclusive package

    Toddler care included · ages 1–3Cleanliness 4.8

    Toddlers ’N’ Casa is fully supervised for ages 1–3, with full days from 8:30am to 4pm including lunch and one counsellor per three children. The best cleanliness scores of the five and a calm, roped-off beach with lifeguards.

    Watch out: it is all-inclusive only through the Casa Inclusive package, premium restaurants cost extra, and no seller bundles flights with a private transfer. A July 2026 guest had building-site noise next to the room.

  3. 3

    Best-reviewed all-inclusive

    Club Med Michès Playa Esmeralda

    Miches, Dominican Republic · La Perla family suites

    Petit Club Med · extra feeTripAdvisor 4.7

    The highest guest scores of the classic all-inclusives (4.7 from 5,686 TripAdvisor reviews) and a purpose-built daycare for 2–3-year-olds with nap rooms and changing rooms.

    Watch out: Petit Club Med costs extra and must be bought for the whole stay. The resort is 75–105 minutes from Punta Cana airport, the surf is Atlantic, and Dec 2024–Jan 2025 reviews describe flooding and mildew in ground-floor rooms.

  4. 4

    Shortest transfer

    Club Med Punta Cana

    Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · 2023 family rooms

    Petit Club Med · extra fee5–10 min from PUJ

    The same Petit Club Med 5–10 minutes from the airport, with family rooms renovated from 2023 and a new food hall and lobby from October 2025.

    Watch out: Christmas falls in the French school holidays. Reviewers describe a French-speaking majority and a kids club that has run in French. Unrenovated rooms draw mold and hygiene complaints.

  5. 5

    Newest in the Dominican Republic

    Family Selection at Grand Palladium Select Bávaro

    Bávaro, Punta Cana · Family Selection tier

    Baby Club drop-off unconfirmedReopened Dec 2025

    A US$61.5 million renovation reopened in December 2025 with a premium family tier, a family butler and a free Baby Club for ages 1–3 with a nap room.

    Watch out: no source confirms you can leave a toddler at the Baby Club, and the hotel says under-18s must always be accompanied. First-year reviews mention perks “never provided” and cockroaches.

With 38 days to go, keep two options open

Ask Beaches Turks & Caicos (Treasure Beach butler suite) and Casa de Campo (Premier Suite) for availability and a quote at the same time, and keep both open until the written answers come back. Check whether each deposit is refundable before you pay it. At Christmas the scarce thing for a toddler family is a staffed childcare place for an under-3, not a suite: Club Med sells those places in advance and Casa de Campo includes them in its package, while a free nursery has to be confirmed rather than assumed.

Beaches Turks & Caicos

Grace Bay, Providenciales, Turks & Caicos · book the new Treasure Beach Village

PLS 15–20 min Free nursery · confirm a 30-month-old Grace Bay · calm New village · Mar 2026 TripAdvisor 4.3 · #34 of 52

The only finalist that puts the newest family rooms, the best toddler beach and a resort-bundled private airport car into one booking. Treasure Beach Village, a US$150 million sixth village of 101 suites, opened on March 1, 2026 with a pool, waterslide and splash zone for younger kids. Grace Bay’s water is calm behind an offshore reef and usually free of sargassum. The catches are a line of fine print about the nursery’s age band and the weakest guest reviews of the five, concentrated in the older villages.

Toddler care
Free nursery with certified nanniesThe Infant Program is for “ages 0–2”, but the nursery copy also says “up to 24 months”, and the 3–4 group requires potty training. Open 7:30am–5pm and 6–9pm, closed 12–2pm daily for cleaning. One-time registration on arrival. A private nanny costs extra (2-hour minimum; ≈$15–25 an hour per child, third-party estimate).
How new
Treasure Beach Village, Mar 1, 2026US$150M, 101 suites. The resort opened ≈1997 and every village was refreshed after Hurricane Irma in 2017. Third-party guides say the Caribbean Village east wing (100 rooms) closed Apr 16, 2026 for a rebuild into early 2027.
Room to ask for
Treasure Beach two-bedroom butler suiteFor example the Oceanview Two-Story 2-Bedroom Butler Suite, whose listing includes a “Private Car Airport Transfer”. Ask how far it is from the Caribbean Village works.
Beach
Grace Bay: powder sand, calm waterAn offshore reef keeps the water calm, and Visit TCI names Grace Bay as free of sargassum. No storm had hit the islands in 2026 as of late October. Loungers fill early in peak season.

Casa de Campo

La Romana, Dominican Republic · Casa Inclusive package, Premier Suite

LRM ≈15 min Toddler care included · ages 1–3 Minitas · calm, roped-off Premier Suites · Dec 2022 Cleanliness 4.8

The most certain answer to the childcare requirement and to clean family rooms. Club Med Michès Playa Esmeralda

Miches, Dominican Republic (Atlantic side) · Exclusive Collection

PUJ 75–105 min Petit Club Med · extra fee Soft sand · surf varies Opened Nov 2019 TripAdvisor 4.7

The best-reviewed classic all-inclusive of the five, with a purpose-built daycare for 2–3-year-olds: Petit Club Med has nap rooms, changing rooms and its own kitchen. It opened in November 2019 as Club Med’s first Exclusive Collection resort in the Americas, so it is fairly new rather than newly renovated. The bad reviews cluster around water. Heavy rain in Dec 2024–Jan 2025 brought flooding, sewage smells and mildew to ground-floor rooms, and Miches gets more rain than Punta Cana even in the dry months.

Club Med Punta Cana

Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · ask for a 2023-renovated family room

PUJ 5–10 min Petit Club Med · extra fee Shallow kids’ area · lifeguards Family rooms 2023 · food hall 2025 TripAdvisor 4.4

The same Petit Club Med as Michès, with the easiest logistics in the field: the resort sits 2.7 km from Punta Cana airport. It opened in 1981 but has been renovated in waves, with 90 two-bedroom family rooms from July 2023 and, in October 2025, a new El Mundo food hall, lobby lounge, bar, coffee shop and 40 renovated rooms. Two risks keep it fourth: room quality depends on which building you get, and Christmas falls in the French school holidays.

Toddler care
Petit Club Med, ages 2–3, extra fee8:45am–5:30pm plus 7–9pm, six days a week (agency listing); limited places, so book it with the room; parents supply diapers. A family paid ≈US$444 per child for 7 nights (≈2025) and used it for naps, lunch and snack.
How new
Family rooms 2023, public areas Oct 2025Opened 1981; a fire closed it from Sep to Nov 9, 2024. October 2025 added El Mundo Food Club, Buena Vista Lounge, Joia Bar, a coffee shop and 40 Costa del Coco Deluxe rooms.
Room to ask for
A 2023-renovated two-bedroom family roomOr two connecting renovated Family Superior rooms (≈732 sq ft together). Get “renovated” written on the booking; unrenovated rooms draw the mold complaints.

Others considered

Age rules, closures and tired rooms knocked out 50-plus resorts

Most of the field failed on the age rule alone. Three near-misses in Jamaica each beat a top-five resort on one point.

Beaches Negril, Jamaica

The same included nursery as Turks & Caicos and a calm, shallow Seven Mile Beach. But it dates from 1997, reopened on Dec 6, 2025 after only “debris removal and repairs” from Hurricane Melissa, and 2026 reviewers call its rooms “dark and dated”. It’s 80–95 minutes from Montego Bay. The best Jamaica fallback if older rooms are acceptable.

FDR Franklyn D. Resort, Jamaica

The best childcare on paper: every family gets its own Vacation Nanny, 8:30am–4:40pm daily, at no extra charge, and children under 6 stay free on the Dec 22–Jan 5 offer (3-night minimum). But reviewers call the rooms “a bit outdated”, hurricane repairs ran into 2026, and the beach is a calm but “tiny and rocky” cove.

Half Moon, Jamaica

Jamaica’s most freshly restored resort (its Eclipse wing reopened Apr 2, 2026), 10 minutes from Montego Bay airport and rated 4.7 on TripAdvisor. But its children’s village takes only potty-trained kids aged 3 and up, so a toddler needs a paid nanny ($30 for the first two hours, then $15 per half hour), and agents report 8–12-night festive minimum stays.

How this was researched

Method and limits

Research ran on November 17, 2026, 38 days before arrival. About 60 all-inclusives on the Caribbean islands were screened against your five requirements, starting with whether a 2½-year-old can be left in staffed care without a parent. Mexico, including Cancún and the Riviera Maya, was left out. The finalists were then checked for renovations, beach conditions, booking rules, Christmas pricing and 2025–26 guest reviews.

The ranking follows your requirements in the order you gave them: all-inclusive and family-friendly, toddler care, new or renovated rooms, the beach, then air and a private transfer. Bad reviews count against each resort.
